About neversleep.

neversleep. is a Youngstown, Ohio based metalcore project blending cinematic heaviness with emotional honesty. Launched in 2024, the project is led by vocalist Mason Edmunds, guitarist Alan Bryan, and drummer Shawn Lauderback. Together, they fuse low-tuned aggression, electronic elements, and post-hardcore vulnerability into a sound that feels intimate and explosive.

Rather than staying confined to one sound, neversleep. operates within a fluid writing structure that prioritizes substance over style. This approach has resulted in a growing catalog of over a dozen tracks and many more in production. Each track is built with intention, balancing modern production with a sense of urgency that never feels manufactured.

With the upcoming Living While You’re Dead EP on the horizon, neversleep. continues to build momentum. Plans for 2026 include the release of the EP alongside a debut slate of live performances, bringing their sound from the studio to the stage.
